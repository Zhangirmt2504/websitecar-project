Frequently asked questions

How long is a test drive?

A standard test drive takes about 30 minutes on city roads with one of our consultants.

What documents do I need for a test drive?

Bring your ID and a valid driving licence. You must be at least 21 years old.

Can I compare prices before visiting?

Yes. All starting prices are in the comparison table on the Cars & Services page.

Does the form on this page book a real test drive?

No. This website is a static student project. The form only shows how browser validation works and then opens a demonstration page. Nothing is sent or saved.
