About Aurelius Cars

A showroom built on trust

We started as a small family garage and grew into a showroom with six car models and our own service centre. Our goal has not changed: help every customer find a car they will enjoy for many years.

Our story

  1. 2014 The founders open a small garage for car repair and tyre service.
  2. 2017 We start selling checked used cars with a written guarantee.
  3. 2020 The new showroom opens with sports cars, SUVs and sedans.
  4. 2023 We add electric cars and a fast charging station.
  5. 2026 Our own service centre and detailing studio open next to the showroom.
